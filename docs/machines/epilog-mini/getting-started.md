# Getting Started

This guide introduces the basic workflow for using the
Epilog Mini 24 at Fab Lab Ísafjörður.

!!! warning "Safety"
    Never leave the laser cutter unattended while
    operating. Only use approved materials and ensure
    the ventilation system is working correctly.

## Before you begin

- Check that the machine is clean and ready.
- Check that the ventilation system is on and working.
- Make sure your material is suitable for laser cutting.
- Check that the material fits inside the working area.
- Inspect the machine for any visible problems.

## Preparing your design

Prepare your design using suitable design software.

Check the dimensions and distinguish between cutting
and engraving operations.

## Finishing

- Wait until the job has finished.
- Ensure the material is safe to handle.
- Remove your work and any remaining material.
- Clean the working area if necessary.
